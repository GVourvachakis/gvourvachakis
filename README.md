# Hi, I'm Georgios 👋🏼

**Research software for noisy data and stiff equations.**

I'm **Georgios Vourvachakis (Vakis)**, an independent researcher working across probabilistic machine learning, stochastic simulation, and numerical methods. I completed my MSc in Materials Science & Engineering (Computational Materials Science) at the University of Crete, with research at IACM and IESL, FORTH.

## Selected work

- **[TimeCausalVQVAE](https://github.com/GVourvachakis/TimeCausalVQVAE)** — Extending Time-Causal VAE baselines with causal VQ/RVQ tokenizers, autoregressive token priors, and path-risk diagnostics for market generation. [Python package](https://pypi.org/project/time-causal-vae/).
- **[exponax-spde](https://github.com/GVourvachakis/exponax-spde)** — Stochastic Allen–Cahn solvers built on Exponax, with exponential Euler–Maruyama integration, Q-Wiener noise, ensemble utilities, and numerical validation in JAX.

## MSc research

**Surrogate Modelling for Damage Threshold Estimation in Ultrashort Laser-Metal Interactions**

Developed linear and nonlinear two-temperature-model solvers, including an IMEX Heun–Crank–Nicolson scheme with a strong Gauss–Seidel predictor, supported by stiffness and convergence studies. Built machine-learning surrogates for damage-threshold estimation.

[Read the thesis](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21397082) · [Thesis and presentation source](https://github.com/GVourvachakis/surrogate-damage-threshold-thesis)
